Rocket Catch Kids Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Rocket Catch Kids is published by Stanley Leong, an individual developer. The game is designed for preschool children under age 5, with parents and guardians responsible for setup and supervision.

Information handled by the game

Rocket Catch Kids does not require an account and does not send gameplay, progress, settings, motion readings, or player profile information to a server operated by the publisher. The game contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Campaign progress and preferences are stored in the device's app storage. The operating system or a device-backup service may handle app data according to the device and backup settings. Motion readings are used temporarily to steer the rocket during play and are not retained by the game.

The game currently has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Dedication media

The game includes a dedication screen containing the first name Evan and a family-supplied photo. The photo is bundled with the app and displayed as part of the game. The game does not collect it from players or upload it. The publisher retains the appropriate guardian permissions and rights for its use.

Theme songs

Theme songs play in the game. The app does not provide a song download or export feature.

Retention and choices

The game keeps progress and preferences in app storage until the app's data is cleared or the app is removed. Platform backups may retain a copy under the device owner's backup settings. The publisher has no player account or server record to access or delete. Users can clear local app data or remove the app using device settings.

Parents and guardians

Parents or guardians with questions about this policy or the game's privacy practices can contact the publisher at rocketcatchkids@gmail.com.

The policy is available offline inside the game. Opening this website or an email app from the game requires a separate grown-up math check, even when mission checks are disabled. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose servers may receive standard web request data such as an IP address. Emails sent to support are handled by the sender's email provider and Gmail and include the sender's address and any information they choose to send. Please do not include children's personal information in support messages.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the app-store privacy disclosures will be updated.
